Care for a mechanical timepiece.

Mechanical watches reward thoughtful handling. The right advice depends on the individual watch and its condition.

Follow the care instructions supplied with your specific timepiece. Avoid strong impacts, and do not assume that a watch is water-resistant unless its documentation clearly says so. If you have questions about winding, storage or servicing, ask the atelier before making a change to your routine.

For a watch that needs attention, share its reference and describe what you have noticed. Our team can help you understand the next step.

A few useful answers.

For anything specific to your order or timepiece, a direct conversation is best.

How do I pay for an online order?

The shop currently offers cash on delivery. Your order and delivery details are confirmed with you by phone.

When will my order arrive?

Delivery arrangements are confirmed with you by the atelier after checkout. Contact us if you need to discuss timing before placing an order.

Terms can depend on the timepiece and order. Please contact the atelier for the applicable details before you purchase; we do not want you to rely on assumptions. See our delivery and returns guidance for more information.

Can I ask about care or servicing?

Yes. Send the reference, your question and any relevant details to our atelier. We will help identify the right next step.
