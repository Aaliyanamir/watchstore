Hand-finished · Assembled with intention

The details are the point.

The character of a mechanical watch lives in the work most people will never see. At AURELUX, finishing is part of how every component earns its place.

Three finishes. One point of view.

Each technique creates its own texture and reflection. Together, they give the AH-01 movement a quiet depth that reveals itself as the light moves.

01 · 3 HOURS PER MOVEMENT

Côtes de Genève

Fine waves are drawn across each bridge with a boxwood wheel, one pass at a time. The stripes are aligned from bridge to bridge for a continuous pattern.

02 · 4,000 CIRCLES

Perlage

A rotating peg creates overlapping circles across the mainplate. Each impression is made individually, building a surface that catches the light.

03 · 64 INWARD ANGLES

Anglage

Edges are filed to forty-five degrees and polished with gentian wood. The AH-01's inward angles are completed by hand, where a machine cannot reach.

A patient process, from start to finish.

Time at the bench is only one part of the work. Assembly, regulation and final finishing each have their own place in the process.

01 · ASSEMBLY

Bring every part together

The movement is assembled so that the relationship between its parts can be checked as a complete mechanism.

02 · REGULATION

Observe across six positions

Each AH-01 is regulated in six positions over three weeks before it is taken down for its final finishing.

03 · FINAL BUILD

Finish, then assemble again

After finishing, the same watchmaker brings the movement together for its final inspection.
