AURELUX · Calibre AH-01 · Genève

A movement with nothing to hide.

The AH-01 is a hand-wound skeleton tourbillon. Its architecture brings the mechanics into view, making the passage of time part of the experience.

Designed around the movement.

Every visible bridge, wheel and spring has a role. Follow the calibre from the energy stored in its barrel to the regulated beat at its centre.

A closer look at the architecture.

Explore the parts that transform a turn of the crown into the steady motion of the hands.

01 · ENERGY

The barrel

A coiled mainspring stores the energy supplied at the crown. Its reserve is designed to keep the calibre running for up to 72 hours when fully wound.

02 · TRANSMISSION

The going train

A sequence of precisely proportioned wheels carries power through the movement and drives the display of hours and minutes.

03 · REGULATION

The tourbillon

The balance, hairspring and escapement travel together in a cage that completes one turn each minute.

Specifications, without the shorthand.

The numbers behind the AH-01 describe a compact, hand-wound calibre assembled for a clear view of its mechanics.

32.4 mm calibre

BEAT
