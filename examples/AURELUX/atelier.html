A small atelier. A personal standard.

AURELUX is built around a simple idea: make fewer watches, give each one the attention it deserves, and let the work speak for itself.

Watchmaking at a considered pace.

We choose a small annual production so each timepiece can be guided closely from the first file stroke to the final screw.

Our atelier works in the tradition of hands-on watchmaking: careful assembly, considered finishing and a close look at the details that keep a mechanical movement in motion. Each AH-01 is regulated in six positions over three weeks, then taken down for its final finishing before it is assembled again.

The person who completes that final build signs beneath the balance bridge. It is a quiet mark of responsibility, made for the next watchmaker who looks inside.

A team of six. Time for the details.

Small by design, the atelier keeps its attention on the workbench and the people who will wear each watch.
