01 · PLACE AN ORDER

Share accurate details

Provide a reachable mobile number and complete delivery address at checkout. Please review them before submitting your order.

02 · CONFIRM

Speak with the atelier

Orders currently use cash on delivery. Our team will contact you to confirm the order and discuss the delivery arrangements for your location.

03 · DELIVERY

Know what to expect

Delivery timing and any location-specific details are confirmed with you directly. Please contact us before ordering if you need to check an arrangement.

Questions about a return?

Return and exchange arrangements can depend on the watch and order. Please ask the atelier about the terms that apply to your purchase.

If you have a concern about an order or received timepiece, contact us with your order details before sending anything back. We will explain the appropriate next step and any applicable conditions.

Please review the terms provided with your order and ask any questions before completing a purchase. This page is general guidance; it does not replace the terms confirmed for an individual order.
